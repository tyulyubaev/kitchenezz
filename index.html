Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about our independent Wren kitchen installation service.

Can you install a kitchen purchased directly from Wren?

Yes. You can purchase your kitchen directly from Wren and use KitchenEzz as your independent installation team. We work from your Wren kitchen design and can provide either a dry-fit installation or manage the complete kitchen project, including preparation works.

How much does Wren kitchen installation cost?

The price depends on the size and complexity of your kitchen and the preparation work required. We review your kitchen design before providing a clear quotation for the agreed works.

What is included in your kitchen installation service?

We offer two options: Dry Fit Installation and Complete Kitchen Installation. A complete project can include removal of your old kitchen, disposal, plumbing, electrics, gas works where required, kitchen fitting, appliance installation, worktops, flooring, tiling and plastering.

What is a dry fit kitchen installation?

Dry fit is suitable when the kitchen has already been fully prepared for installation. Plumbing, electrics, walls, flooring and other preparation work should be completed correctly before our kitchen fitter starts.

Can you remove and dispose of my old kitchen?

Yes. Kitchen removal and disposal can be included as part of your quotation, so you don't need to arrange a separate contractor before installation.

Can you do the plumbing and electrical work?

Yes. For complete kitchen projects, we can arrange the plumbing and electrical work required for the new kitchen, including appliance connections, sockets, cooker points and alterations to water and waste pipes.

Do you connect kitchen appliances?

Yes. Appliance installation and connections can be included in your quotation, depending on the appliances and the scope of your project.

How long does a kitchen installation take?

It depends on the kitchen and the amount of preparation required. A straightforward installation may take several days, while a complete renovation involving removal, plumbing, electrics, plastering, flooring or tiling will take longer. We discuss the expected schedule before work begins.

Which areas of London do you cover?

We install kitchens across London, including North, East, South East, South West and West London. Send us your postcode and we can confirm coverage for your property.

How do I get a quotation?

Simply send us your postcode, Wren kitchen design and photos of your current kitchen. This allows us to understand the installation requirements and prepare your quotation. You can contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email at quote@kitchenezz.co.uk.
